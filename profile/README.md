# Torinel™

**German financial intelligence on banking, AI, technology and financial decision-making.**

Torinel is an independent research and analysis platform focused on how banking systems, financial products and financial infrastructure are changing.

## Current research

### Torinel AI Banking Index™

The Torinel AI Banking Index™ examines how far customer-facing banking AI moves from:

**Information → Decision → Execution → Delegation → Control**

The 2026 edition currently covers:

- Commerzbank Ava
- ING AgenticAI
- DKB KI Support
- N26 KI-Insights
- Postbank Paula

Institutional factual verification is currently in progress. No preliminary rankings are published.

## Research principles

Torinel separates:

- documented public evidence
- institutional verification
- editorial assessment
- unresolved questions

Bank responses are treated as evidence input, not as approval of Torinel methodology or conclusions.
